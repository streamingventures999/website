About us

Before Streaming Ventures was a label, it was two friends making music together.

Daniel and Raphael spent years producing tracks side by side, mostly for the joy of it. Slowly the songs piled up, and we noticed people were using them the way we did: to focus, to slow down, to get through the long hours.

In 2023 we turned that into Streaming Ventures, a small independent label in Berlin for music that does its work quietly in the background.

We didn’t do it alone. Our producer Franco worked with us on production, and a lot of what you hear carries his touch. We still make the music ourselves, and we still make it together.

Streaming Ventures is a label of Weschta & von Hoch GbR.
